# Plan map export: a view-only picture of where the project stands (research, 2026-10-20)

_Research only, nothing built. Asked by the owner (chat, 2026-10-20): improve the plan map export so it shows tasks and subtasks in a sensible, view-only form, like today's slide picture (no pan, no zoom), but showing the project's current status (done / open / blocked / late, subtasks, links). Coverage: 11 web searches and 1 page read (Mermaid docs); most findings come from search summaries of help pages and forum posts, not full reads, and are marked as such. Sources were not entered in a claims register._

## Today (from the code)
- `src/planning/plan-slide.ts`: `slideModel` (compact: Done tiles / next N columns as cards with arrows / Later and Not linked tiles; or the whole map) and `slideSvg` (1920 × 1080, fixed light palette). A card shows the title, due date, priority, subtask count, result and experiment. Subtasks appear as a strip under the card (✓ / ○, "+N more").
- `src/planning/plan-slide-ui.ts`: the "Picture for a slide" window. It has a preview and Copy picture (PNG on the clipboard, desktop only). Nothing is saved, so a pasted picture is out of date as soon as a to-do changes.
- Status the picture does **not** show: late (the due date is printed but not compared with today), waiting (`waiting` is read in `plan.ts:142` only to work out `blocked`, and is not kept on `PlanRow`), why a card is not ready, the date the picture was made, overall progress, and a key to the borders.

## Pros and cons for Lab Kit
- **Picture window only, with more status** (smallest change). Pro: one file (`plan-slide.ts`) plus a field on `PlanRow`. Con: the owner still has to re-copy before each meeting, and nothing in the vault shows the same view.
- **View-only block in a note** (`show: plan-picture`). Pro: always current, and it suits meeting notes and hubs. It draws an `<img>` of the same SVG (blob URL, as the preview does), so there is no pan, zoom or editing, and `innerHTML` is not needed. Con: a new block to support on phones (picture only, no copy), and frozen meeting notes need a rule (they should stay a record).
- **Option A, today's layout with status** vs **Option B, columns**: A keeps the arrows and the "Done and later as tiles" choice the owner already tested. B is simpler to read but drops the dependency shape (interpretation).
- **Readability** stays a number: `minText` and the warning under 22 px are kept. Status words get their own line so they are not cut by the title.

## Recommendation (for the owner to decide; nothing chosen)
1. One engine (`plan-slide.ts`) feeds both the window and a new **view-only block**. Copy picture stays desktop only.
2. Layout **Option A**: today's compact layout, plus on each card a status line ("late: due 15 Oct", "waiting: facility reply", "after GPC analysis", "due 23 Oct (in 3 d)"), a red border for late and an amber one for waiting, "2/4 subtasks", an "as of" date, "N of M done" and a one-line key.
3. Status is computed only from what the notes say: late = open and the due date is before today; waiting = the `waiting` property; not ready = the open to-dos it comes after. There is no "on track" verdict.
4. A frozen meeting note turns the block into a plain list of the to-dos with their status, like the other frozen lists. Nothing new is saved in the vault (the export decision of 2026-10-13: "Nothing saved in the vault").
